# Phase 9A - the screening reframe: both arms re-evaluated as a triage task

Generated by `scripts/screening_reframe_report_phase9a.py` from `data/interim/phase9a/screening.json`.
**Every threshold, operating-point criterion and margin in this report was declared
in CLAUDE.md section 6 and committed (`755da9b`) before any of it ran.**

## Why this phase exists

Every gate in this project was written in mean absolute error, g/dL. The product a
screening tool delivers is a **binary referral decision**, which is a different task
with different metrics. This phase tests the recorded refutations on the product's
own terms. It was not an attempt to rescue them, and one recorded claim did not
survive - see the correction in section 1.

## 1. THE COMPARISON THAT DECIDES IT - model versus the cheap baseline

The question is never whether the model has skill. It is whether it has skill
**beyond a demographic baseline**, on the screening task, at an operating point
chosen without seeing the test labels.

Operating point, pre-declared: **the highest specificity subject to sensitivity
≥ 0.90, chosen on the training folds only** and applied to the held-out fold.
Margin, pre-declared: **≥ 0.10** improvement with a **bootstrap CI lower bound
above zero**, in either direction (sensitivity at matched specificity, or
specificity at matched sensitivity - the pre-declaration says these count equally).

### 3b. Cross-site - the decisive failure, and it is not a knife-edge

**Counts first, rates second, and no rate without its interval.** Phase 9D found
this the least precisely estimated comparison in the project, so the observed
counts - which carry no estimation uncertainty at all - lead, and every rate below
carries a 95% subject-level bootstrap CI (the specificity, PPV and referral-rate
intervals were computed in Phase 9E; Phase 9A stored only the sensitivity one).

| direction | n test | anaemic | flagged | MAE (recorded) | bias | AUROC (95% CI) | sens (95% CI) | spec (95% CI) | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| italy_to_india | 95 | 68 | **27 of 68** | 1.914 (1.958) | +1.551 | 0.764 [0.657, 0.857] | **0.397 [0.278, 0.514]** | 0.963 [0.885, 1.000] | **NOT USEFUL** |
| india_to_italy | 121 | 23 | **21 of 23** | 1.882 (1.995) | -1.450 | 0.883 [0.800, 0.956] | **0.913 [0.783, 1.000]** | 0.418 [0.323, 0.516] | **NOT USEFUL** |

The operating point is chosen on the **training site** and applied to the held-out
site, which is the only honest construction available. It transfers badly:
`italy_to_india` flags **27 of 68** anaemic subjects - sensitivity 0.397 [0.278, 0.514] - because
the +1.55 g/dL bias pushes predictions above the train-site cut. `india_to_italy`
flags 21 of 23 anaemic subjects, sensitivity 0.913 [0.783, 1.000], but at specificity 0.418 [0.323, 0.516] - below the
pre-declared 0.50 floor, and its whole interval is below that floor.

**What is certain here and what is not.** The *direction* of the collapse is
directly observed and is not in doubt: 41 of 68 anaemic subjects went unflagged in
one direction, and that is a count, not an estimate. The *magnitude* is another
matter. `italy_to_india` specificity rests on **27 non-anaemic subjects** and
Phase 9D measured only 14% power on that direction, so the size of the cross-site
penalty must not be quoted as a precise quantity. Phase 9E gives the sample that
would pin it down (`reports/phase9e_boundaries.md`).

**Checked for seed dependence rather than assumed.** Per-seed cross-site operating
points give sensitivity 0.250 / 0.412 / 0.426 for `italy_to_india` and
sensitivity 0.957 / 0.957 / 0.826 at specificity 0.449 / 0.378 / 0.602 for
`india_to_italy`. **No seed satisfies both floors in either direction**, so the
cross-site verdict does not rest on the non-determinism documented in section 6.

### Verdict per arm

**Imaging: WEAKENED within site, CONFIRMED cross-site.** The recorded clause
"moves no clinical threshold" is contradicted for the site-mixed regime and is
corrected. The arm remains not deployable, but the binding reason is now the
cross-site collapse - 41 of 68 anaemic subjects missed in one direction
(sensitivity 0.397, CI [0.278, 0.514]); 57 of 98 non-anaemic subjects needlessly
flagged in the other (specificity 0.418, CI [0.323, 0.516]) - rather than the
absence of a within-site effect. Under this project's own
hard constraint - *single-site accuracy numbers are considered worthless* - that
binding reason is the one that governs.

**PPG: CONFIRMED, and strengthened.** No condition clears any margin; the selected
deep model is significantly worse than demographics at matched sensitivity; its NNS
is worse than referring everybody. Phase 7's plug-in sensitivity 0.000 reproduces
exactly, now correctly labelled as a plug-in artefact rather than a discriminative
ceiling. **The PPG verdict rests on its MAE and NNS figures, which are well
powered (MDE 0.137 g/dL), and not on its AUROC comparison, whose MDE is 0.270 on
18 anaemic subjects.** An AUROC difference of -0.000 on that sample is not
evidence of equivalence; it is an uninformative measurement, and is reported as
one.

### How precisely these numbers are known (added by Phase 9E)

Phase 9D measured what this design could have detected, and the answer differs
sharply by comparison. **The two regression comparisons are ADEQUATELY POWERED** -
MDE 0.181 g/dL for imaging and 0.137 for PPG, against a narrowest WHO band of 1.0 -
so those negative results are informative evidence of absence and keep their full
strength. **Three comparisons here are UNDERPOWERED and their nulls are bounded,
not empty:** within site no specificity gain of 0.10 or larger was detected and
the design could have detected 0.116 (67% power at the margin); the same applies
to the sensitivity direction at 0.114. Cross-site, the MDE is 0.314 for
italy_to_india (14% power) and 0.165 for india_to_italy (40%). **This bounds what
the null comparisons mean; it does not touch the observed collapse**, which is a
count of subjects, not an estimate. Phase 9E converts each underpowered comparison
into a required sample size - see `reports/phase9e_boundaries.md`.

## 5. WHO thresholds, and which subjects they were applied to

Both datasets are **entirely adult** - Eyes-Defy ages 19-88, Hb-PPG ages 21-90,
**no subject under 19** - so **no child threshold applies to anyone** and none was
used. `hemosight.evaluation.screening.who_threshold` raises rather than applying an
adult threshold to a subject under 15.

| group | threshold | Eyes-Defy | Hb-PPG |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| men ≥ 15 y | Hb < 13.0 g/dL | applied | applied |
| non-pregnant women ≥ 15 y | Hb < 12.0 g/dL | applied | applied |
| children (6-59 mo / 5-11 y / 12-14 y) | 11.0 / 11.5 / 12.0 | none present | none present |

Resulting prevalence: Eyes-Defy **91 of 216 (0.421)**, Hb-PPG **18 of 252 (0.071)**.

> **Limitation, declared before running and not resolvable from the data.**
> **Pregnancy status is not recorded in either dataset.** The non-pregnant
> threshold is applied to every woman. A pregnant woman's WHO threshold is 11.0
> g/dL, so any pregnant subject is over-called anaemic in every number above.

## 6. Limitations and reproducibility

1. **The knife-edges are real and are not resolved by this data.** The pooled CNN
   misses the sensitivity floor by 0.010 (0.890 against 0.900, bootstrap CI
   [0.822, 0.950]) and the specificity margin by 0.004. The colour model clears the
   margin but misses the same floor by 0.010. With 91 anaemic subjects the boundary
   cannot be resolved; the verdict labels should not be read as decisive, and the
   cross-site result should.
2. **Pilot scale.** Eyes-Defy is 216 subjects, one image each, one Samsung Galaxy S6
   in two regional variants, two sites, no severe cases. Hb-PPG is 252 subjects,
   one site, one device, **18 anaemic subjects** - every PPG interval here is wide
   for that reason alone.
3. **The operating point was chosen on training folds only**, so the held-out
   sensitivity lands near but not exactly on the 0.90 target: all 10/10 folds
   reached the target in training, and held-out sensitivity is 0.879-0.901.
4. **`population_mean` is a constant predictor**, so its AUROC (0.451 imaging, 0.414 PPG)
   is an artefact of 10 slightly different train-fold means breaking ties, not a
   ranking. Its screening row is the honest one: it refers everybody.
5. **Regenerated, because it was never saved.** Phase 4 Gate B and Phase 6.5 Task 2
   stored only summary metrics, so every **baseline** per-subject prediction here was
   regenerated with the same estimators on the identical folds
   (`KFold(10, shuffle=True, random_state=20260911)`, verified identical across both
   PPG scripts). The four-wavelength PPG predictions were reused from the Phase 6
   known-truth files and reproduce the recorded MAEs to 0.0000.
6. **Cross-site CNN non-determinism, measured not assumed.** The cross-site
   predictions had to be regenerated (only MAE was stored). Two independent runs of
   the identical seeded construction gave seed-averaged MAE 1.863 then 1.914
   (`italy_to_india`) and 1.978 then 1.882 (`india_to_italy`) - a spread of
   0.05-0.10 g/dL, and up to 0.167 per seed. That is an order of magnitude larger
   than the ~0.01 tolerance documented for the deep sweep. The cross-site path
   trains a single model on one site with no fold averaging, and no cuDNN
   determinism flags are set in this project; **the cause was not isolated and is
   not recorded as established.** All observed values stay inside the MARGINAL band
   and no seed satisfies both screening floors, so no verdict moves.
