## 1. TASK 1 - the empirical colour-per-haemoglobin signal, re-measured

### What was on record

`scripts/phase3_report.py` carried the string **"~0.70 dE2000 per g/dL, 216 Eyes-Defy subjects, binned by haemoglobin, n=3 lowest bin excluded"** with no producing script and no output file. It was the numerator of the 5.6x noise-to-signal ratio.

### What the measurement found

`scripts/phase3_empirical_signal.py` -> `data/interim/phase3/empirical_signal.json` (215 subjects; 2 lack a palpebral mask). Per-subject palpebral colour from the dataset's own mask (three mask encodings were found and handled: alpha cutout, white-background cutout, 3-channel), mean linear-sRGB luminance-trimmed 5-95%, CIELAB D65, as captured.

| estimator | dE2000 per g/dL | null (Hb shuffled, 500x) | p | reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| binned by Hb (the method on record) | 2.29 | 2.13 +/- 0.46 | 0.33 | **cannot measure the signal** - returns ~2.1 with no Hb signal at all |
| regression, Hb + site | 1.37 (CI 1.06-1.71) | 0.17 | 0.002 | upper bound - counts sex-linked colour as Hb (r(Hb, male) = 0.55) |
| **regression, Hb + site + sex + age (HEADLINE)** | **0.84** (CI 0.57-1.17) | 0.13 | 0.002 | the Hb-specific signal; still an upper bound |

Per site (adjusted): India 0.68, Italy 0.92. Grey-world on a tight crop: 0.78. Between-subject colour at FIXED Hb, sex, age and site: **4.55 dE2000** = 5.4 g/dL equivalent - the real data's own noise floor, under the dataset's own fixed white LED, before any capture-condition noise.

### The verdict on the old figure, and on the gate

**The 0.70 figure is WITHDRAWN as a measurement, not corrected.** The method it was attributed to has a null of ~2.1 dE2000/g/dL: CIEDE2000 between noisy bin means measures the noise in the means. Whatever it read, it was never a measurement. That the adjusted regression (0.84, CI 0.57-1.17) brackets 0.70 is coincidence and is not treated as confirmation.

**Recomputed downstream quantities.** Nothing was adjusted to match.

| quantity | was | now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| empirical signal | ~0.70 | 0.84 (CI 0.57-1.17) |
| noise/signal at 3.935 dE2000 (grey-world, 25% FOV) | 5.6x | 4.7x (CI 3.4-6.9) |
| noise/signal at 6.076 (full frame) | 8.7x | 7.3x |
| equivalent Hb error at 3.935, empirical | 5.6 g/dL | 4.7 g/dL (CI 3.4-6.9); 2.9 even with the unadjusted upper-bound signal |
| model vs empirical | 'within ~1.5x, model slightly conservative' | model underestimates by 1.9x (CI 1.3-2.6x) |
| Phase 3 Task 0 gate (simulated inversion) | 3.893 g/dL NOT RECOVERABLE | 3.893 g/dL, unchanged - computed on the model, not on this figure |

**Does any verdict change? No.** The Phase 3 gate verdict was computed on the simulated inversion and is untouched. The empirical cross-check that supported it still supports it: 4.7 g/dL equivalent error at the measured residual, above the pre-declared 2.0 g/dL NOT RECOVERABLE line at the central estimate, at both CI bounds (3.4-6.9), and even under the unadjusted upper-bound signal (2.9). Phases 2, 2.5 and 3.5 never used the 0.70 figure: Phase 2/2.5 report dE2000 only, and Phase 3.5 converts with its own measured ratio sensitivity (0.637) and the simulated 0.452. The margin the closure rests on is smaller than was written (4.7x, not 5.6x) and the sim-to-real gap is larger (1.9x, not 1.5x); both are now stated where the old numbers were.

**Two findings the constant could not have produced.** (1) Sex confounds the colour-Hb relationship in Eyes-Defy exactly as it confounded PPG in Phase 4: r(Hb, male) = 0.55, and adjusting for it moves the slope from 1.37 to 0.84. (2) Between-subject conjunctival colour at fixed Hb, sex, age and site is 4.55 dE2000 (5.4 g/dL equivalent) under a fixed white LED with ambient light excluded - a noise floor intrinsic to the tissue and capture, not to calibration, and several times the signal.

**Wired in.** `reproduce_all.py` stage "Phase 3 empirical signal" (deterministic, seed 20260911, ~1 min); `phase3_report.py`, `phase3_5_report.py` and `phase5_final_report.py` read the JSON and refuse to run without it; `tests/test_phase3.py` fails if the report ever holds a copy, if the binned method is presented as a measurement, or if the equivalent error drops below 2.0 g/dL (which would change the verdict and must be reported, not absorbed).
