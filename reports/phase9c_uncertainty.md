**Why five parameters swing by exactly zero, so the table is not misread.** The two melanin *shape* parameters (exponent k, amplitude scale) multiply the melanin volume fraction, and the gate's nominal melanin fraction is **0** - so at nominal they have nothing to act on. Their small Sobol totals say the same thing: they matter only where melanin is present, and then only through it. Epithelium thickness and the water fraction are genuinely negligible (a 32 um layer contributes almost no double-pass attenuation, and water is nearly transparent across 450-700 nm), and the tarsal plate sits below an attenuation floor that its thickness cannot change. **The one-at-a-time and Sobol rankings disagree on the leader** - epithelium BVF one-at-a-time, deep-layer weight by total index - because the one-at-a-time sweep passes through the degenerate point bvf_epi = 0, where the modelled colour stops depending on haemoglobin at all (MAE 10.7 g/dL) and which the joint sample almost never visits. The total index is the quantity to trust; the swing column is shown because it is the form Phase 3 Task 4 used.

### 4b. What the ranking means, and the mechanism behind it

The gate MAE is, to a very good approximation, **colour noise divided by colour signal per g/dL**: across 400 sampled parameter vectors the rank correlation between the gate MAE and the chromaticity distance the model puts between Hb 4 and Hb 18 g/dL is **-0.96**. That span itself varies by an order of magnitude over the prior (median 0.0674, 95% [0.0125, 0.1414]). So the ranking is not mysterious: a parameter matters here exactly to the extent that it changes how much the modelled colour moves per g/dL. This is the same mechanism Phase 3 recorded - signal-to-noise - now measured across the prior instead of at one point.

**On the expectation that melanin dominates: the analysis only partly supports it, and the honest ranking is different.**

* At the Phase 3 residual melanin volume fraction ranks **3 of 14** by total index (ST 0.258), behind **deep-layer contribution weight** (ST 0.303) and epithelium blood volume fraction. At the studio residual it ranks **1**, the largest single contributor (ST 0.319) - melanin matters most precisely where the capture noise is smallest, which is where a real deployment would want to operate.
* Phase 3's finding that melanin is the most consequential assumption was measured in the **mismatch** setting (section 5), where a model calibrated at zero melanin meets a pigmented subject. That result stands and is reproduced here. What this phase adds is that in the **self-consistent** setting - which is the one the gate reports - melanin is one of three or four comparable drivers, not the dominant one.
* The single largest driver at the gate residual is **deep-layer contribution weight**, a **UNSOURCED** modelling constant with no citation at all: the factor 0.6 by which deeper layers are down-weighted in `forward.layered_reflectance`. The second is the epithelial blood volume fraction, also unsourced. **The result is therefore most sensitive to the parts of the model that were never sourced, which is the finding this phase exists to expose.**

**The recommendation the analysis does support.** For anyone continuing this line of work, a measured melanin absorption curve for conjunctival tissue - amplitude and exponent, with inter-individual spread, replacing the fitted power law - is the single most valuable *spectroscopic* measurement, because melanin leads the ranking in the controlled-capture regime and is the mechanism by which the method would be biased by skin tone (N5). But it is **not** the most valuable measurement overall at the uncontrolled residual: there, two unsourced structural quantities - the deep-layer weighting and the epithelial blood volume fraction - matter as much or more, and the first of them is not a tissue property at all but an artefact of how this model combines layers. Fixing a modelling convention costs nothing and would remove the largest single term.

**A limitation that belongs with these numbers.** At the nominal parameters 61% of the modelled (Hb, wavelength) grid is saturated at reflectance >= 0.999, and across the prior the median is 42%. Saturation compresses the colour signal and is part of why the span varies so widely. This is a property of the diffusion approximation as the gate already used it, not something the prior introduced - but it means the absolute MAE values inherit a known model deficiency, and the interval should be read as the spread this model produces, not as the spread of the physical truth.

## 6. Task 4 - the outstanding verification item

The VERIFICATION REQUIRED banner on layer thicknesses and blood volume fractions has stood since Phase 3 because Efron 2009 [5] and Zhivov 2006 [6] were never obtained. With network access both were sought on 2026-09-20.

**Efron 2009 - OBTAINED, and it does not contain the numbers attributed to it.** The paper itself is subscription-only (Optom Vis Sci 86(11):E1303-8, PMID 19770809; Europe PMC `isOpenAccess: N`). The same study - same 11 healthy participants, same instrument, same tissue, abstract matching the published one - is a chapter of the open-access QUT master's thesis it came from (Al Dossari M, *Confocal microscopic examination of the conjunctiva*, QUT ePrints 18316), which was downloaded and read in full. Its palpebral chapter reports **no thickness in micrometres for any layer**: the epithelium "appears to be only two or three cell-layers deep", the stroma is described as "thin" and the tarsal plate as "dark and amorphous", all qualitative. The thesis's only thickness measurement, **32.9 +/- 1.1 um, is of the BULBAR conjunctival epithelium** - a different tissue, in a different chapter, from the companion bulbar study. It also states plainly that "it is therefore not possible to determine the overall thickness of the conjunctiva using this (or any other) technique with any degree of certainty". No blood volume fraction appears anywhere in it.

**Zhivov 2006 - NOT OBTAINED.** The Ocular Surface 4(2):81-93, PMID 16681079, DOI 10.1016/s1542-0124(12)70030-7. Europe PMC: not open access, not in EPMC, no PDF. OpenAlex: `oa_status: closed`, `any_repository_has_fulltext: false`. No author manuscript or repository copy was found. It is a review of confocal microscopy of the ocular surface; its abstract contains no numeric thickness.

**Consequence: the banner is KEPT, and strengthened.** It previously said the values "could not be verified against the source". It can now say something more exact and worse: **for Efron 2009 the source was read and does not contain them.** The stromal thickness (200 um), tarsal-plate thickness (800 um) and all three blood volume fractions have no identified source, and the 32 um epithelial thickness is corroborated by Li et al. 2015 (OCT, 34.0 +/- 5.8 um, palpebral) - which remains the only sourced thickness in the model and is why it is the only parameter here labelled SOURCED. A correction is logged; the citation is not removed, because the paper is still the source of the *qualitative* layer structure the model uses.

Because no value was replaced, there is **no narrowed-prior re-run to report beside the assumed-range one**. The assumed ranges in section 1 are the result. Had Efron 2009 contained the thicknesses, the stroma and tarsal priors would have narrowed - and section 4 shows what that would have been worth: stroma thickness ranks mid-table and tarsal-plate thickness near the bottom, so narrowing them would have moved the interval very little. The parameters that dominate are not the ones the banner is about.

## 7. What changes on the record

**The imaging refutation is strengthened, and should be restated as robust to parameter uncertainty.** At both uncontrolled residuals the entire 95% interval lies above the pre-declared 2.0 g/dL threshold (2.37 and 2.09 at the lower bound), and 99.5% / 98.4% of the prior is NOT RECOVERABLE. No physically plausible combination of the unsourced parameters rescues uncontrolled capture. The verdict did not depend on the fixed parameter values.

**The controlled-capture result is fragile and must be reported as such.** Phase 7 recorded 1.040 g/dL, MARGINAL, sitting 0.04 g/dL above the VIABLE boundary. Under the prior it is median 1.52, 95% [0.60, 4.90] - spanning VIABLE, MARGINAL and NOT RECOVERABLE. **This is a limitation on a result the project already called MARGINAL, not on the central refutation**, but it is a real weakening of the one imaging number that was close to a favourable band, and the write-up must say so plainly rather than quoting 1.04 alone.

**Every appearance of the gate number now carries its interval**, with the point estimate retained and a pointer to this report: `CLAUDE.md` (CURRENT STATE, the section 2 status table rows 3 and 8, and the Phase 3 row of the phase table), `reports/final_results.md` (master table row 3), `reports/phase3_simulation.md` (the headline gate box), `reports/phase7.md` (the gate table and the outcome-B boundary reading), and the front end's case study (`Landing.tsx`) and pre-registration worked example (`PreReg.tsx`). **Nothing was deleted.**

**One target named in the brief turned out not to exist, and is recorded rather than glossed.** The gate number does not appear anywhere in the audit package: the eight checks in `src/hemosight/audit/` carry provenance strings citing Phases 1, 4, 4.5 and 5 (demographic baselines, MI ceilings, duplicate overlap, permutation, seeds, subgroups), because the harness audits a claimed model's predictions and has no simulation stage. There was therefore nothing to update there, and no provenance string was edited to manufacture a mention.

**What this does not establish.** The interval is the spread this forward model produces under a declared prior over its own parameters. It is not a confidence interval on a measurement, it does not cover structural error in the diffusion approximation or the three-layer decomposition, and it holds the illuminant and camera proxy fixed. A wider model class would widen it further; nothing here suggests it would narrow it.
