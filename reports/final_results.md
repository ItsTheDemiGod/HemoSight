# HemoSight - Final Results

Consolidated from every phase. Generated by `scripts/phase5_final_report.py`, reading the interim JSON artefacts directly so the numbers cannot drift from the evidence that produced them.

> ## Summary
>
> **Six representations were tested across two modalities. None produces a usable haemoglobin estimator.** In every case a threshold was declared before the experiment ran.
>
> **The benchmark that beats all six is a single binary variable: the subject's sex, MAE 0.831 g/dL.**
>
> The contribution is the set of negative results and the mechanisms behind them, each measured rather than argued.

## 3. Limitations, gathered from every phase

### Data

* **Only 469 trustworthy haemoglobin measurements exist in the whole project** - 217 Eyes-Defy images and 252 PPG recordings. The Ghana pool's Hb was rejected in Phase 1.5.
* **No severe anemia in any trusted data.** Eyes-Defy minimum 7.0 g/dL, Hb-PPG minimum 8.5. The 48 severe cases that exist are in CP-AnemiC, whose Hb labels conflict on duplicated images (one image carries 10 different values across 8 hospitals).
* **Three genuinely independent sites**, and only two have both full photographs and Hb. CP-AnemiC is contained inside the Ghana conjunctiva set (419 shared MD5 hashes, 87.3% of its files).
* **Device diversity and haemoglobin never co-occur.** MOBIUS has 3 phones and no Hb; Eyes-Defy has Hb and two variants of one phone; Hb-PPG has Hb and one device.
* **Severe age skew across sites:** CP-AnemiC is children 6-60 months, Eyes-Defy adults 19-88, Hb-PPG adults 21-90. Any cross-site transfer changes population and age simultaneously.
* **Headline file counts overstate the data ~2x**: 2,015 unique images among 4,262 Ghana conjunctiva files; 2,097 among 4,260 fingernail files.
* **No ground-truth skin tone labels.** SCIN held no data and had no participant overlap; N5's tone axis would be an ITA proxy confounded with site.

### Statistical power - what these negative results could have detected (Phase 9D)

Minimum detectable effect at 80% power, alpha 0.05, two-sided, on the comparison that actually decided each arm. **Observed (retrospective) power was deliberately not computed** - see `reports/phase9d_power.md`.

* **Imaging regression: ADEQUATELY POWERED.** n=216; MDE **0.18 g/dL** of MAE against the site+sex+age baseline, versus the 1.0 g/dL narrowest WHO band - 100% power to detect a clinically meaningful improvement. The negative result is informative evidence of absence.
* **PPG regression: ADEQUATELY POWERED, and the best-powered comparison in the project.** n=252; MDE **0.14 g/dL** against sex alone, versus 1.0 g/dL. Phase 4.5's negative result is informative.
* **Imaging screening, within site: UNDERPOWERED, marginally.** MDE **0.116** specificity against the 0.10 pre-declared margin (67% power at the margin). **Stated as the bound it is: no specificity gain of 0.10 or larger was detected for the CNN, and this design could have detected 0.116.** That is not the same as no gain existing. The effect the arm did find (+0.192 specificity, mean-CIELAB) was comfortably above its own MDE and is unaffected.
* **Imaging cross-site: UNDERPOWERED, and this is the project's most load-bearing finding.** Per direction: italy->india MDE **0.314** (3.1x the margin, 14% power, specificity estimated on 27 non-anaemic subjects); india->italy MDE **0.165** (40% power). The cross-site *direction* was directly observed - **27 of 68 anaemic subjects flagged, 41 missed**, which is a count and carries no estimation uncertainty - and is not weakened by this. But **the magnitude of the penalty is poorly pinned down and must not be quoted as precise**: the rate behind that count is sensitivity 0.397, 95% CI [0.278, 0.514], and in the other direction specificity 0.418, CI [0.323, 0.516]. Prefer the counts; attach the interval wherever a rate is used.
* **PPG screening AUROC: UNDERPOWERED.** MDE **0.27** AUROC on 18 anaemic subjects (prevalence 7.1%), so the recorded AUROC difference of -0.000 with CI [-0.195, +0.189] is uninformative on its own. The PPG verdict rests on the MAE comparison and the NNS arithmetic, not on AUROC. The PPG *specificity* comparison is adequately powered (MDE 0.073).

**None of this changes any recorded verdict.** It supplies the second half of each negative statement - *and we could have detected an effect of size Y* - which those statements previously lacked.

### The guided-capture gap - the regime this project did not measure (Phase 9E)

**This is the project's primary future-work item, and it is a boundary of the claim rather than an open question.** Phase 7 measured three capture regimes: uncontrolled (3.456 dE2000), one phone and one lighting cell with gaze varying (1.981), and a studio rig (1.062). **A deployed screening app operates in none of them.** Its guided capture is one phone in one session, with a live overlay enforcing framing and distance and a quality gate rejecting blurred or badly exposed frames before the shutter - between the second and third conditions, which is exactly the interval in which the gate crosses bands and exactly where Phase 9C found the verdict fragile.

**It cannot be closed here.** Closing it requires capturing images under that protocol, which section 3 forbids permanently. What Phase 9E does instead is bound it by INTERPOLATION between the two measured points - the geometric mean of the bracketing pair, 1.450 dE2000, declared before the gate was run at it. **This is an interpolation, not a measurement, and is labelled so wherever it appears.** At that point the gate returns **1.44 g/dL, MARGINAL** at nominal parameters, and over the Phase 9C prior **median 2.06, 95% [0.83, 5.73]** - 9% VIABLE, 39% MARGINAL, 52% NOT RECOVERABLE. **VIABLE needs a residual below 0.986 dE2000, below the best measured condition in the whole project, so no point in the guided-capture interval reaches VIABLE - including its most favourable end.** What evidence would settle it is specified in `reports/phase9e_boundaries.md`.

### What a confirmatory study would need (Phase 9E)

Each UNDERPOWERED verdict above, converted into a specification. Required counts are stated for the group that **carries** the metric - non-anaemic subjects for a specificity, anaemic for a sensitivity - because that, not the cohort size, is what failed here.

**Two comparisons carry two figures, and both are shown.** Required n scales as (observed MDE / 0.10) raised to 1/slope, and the slope was measured by subsampling rather than assumed. Where the subsample fit was itself too weak to trust (R2 below 0.50 - which happened on exactly the two smallest carrier groups) the 1/sqrt(n) reference is the headline and the measured-slope figure follows it in brackets. **That R2 floor was added mid-run, after the subsampling** (logged as a deviation, DECISION LOG 2026-09-21), **and it happened to select the more optimistic figure in both cases** - so neither is reported without the other.

| comparison | carrier group | now | needed at 80% | needed at 90% | cohort at the observed prevalence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| imaging within site, specificity | non-anaemic | 125 | **166** | 217 | 286 |
| imaging within site, sensitivity | anaemic | 91 | **120** | 162 | 284 |
| cross-site italy->india, specificity | non-anaemic | 27 | **266** *(measured slope: 778)* | 356 *(measured slope: 1193)* | 936 *(measured slope: 2736)* |
| cross-site india->italy, specificity | non-anaemic | 98 | **311** | 435 | 384 |
| PPG screening, AUROC | anaemic | 18 | **131** *(measured slope: 202)* | 176 *(measured slope: 288)* | 1839 *(measured slope: 2831)* |

**The composition point, which matters more than any total here.** `italy_to_india` estimated specificity on 27 non-anaemic subjects. It needs **266** on the 1/sqrt(n) reference (10x more) or **778** on the measured slope (29x) - the R2 floor (fit R2 0.25) makes the first the headline. At that direction's observed prevalence those are test sites of roughly 936 and 2736 people, against 95 here; recruited to a balanced 50% prevalence, about 532 and 1555. **A study designer should size against the larger unless they can measure the scaling on their own pilot.** Full derivation, the rule, and the R2 that triggered it: `reports/phase9e_boundaries.md`.

### Method and scope

* **The imaging arm's conjunctiva datasets have no sclera** - they are pre-segmented cutouts (least-cropped image still 49.7% black), so N1 could only be tested end-to-end on Eyes-Defy's 218 subjects.
* **Task 4's crop analysis is idealised**: crops are centred on the eye using the segmentation, so hand-framing noise was not simulated.
* **MOBIUS has no conjunctiva segmentation**, so the ratio pair the deployed method would use (conjunctiva/sclera) could not be tested; iris/sclera is the proxy.
* **Gate A's device-invariance premise is untestable** on Hb-PPG: one device for all 252 subjects.
* **Layer thicknesses and blood volume fractions remain hand-transcribed** from the literature; only the spectral constants are now file-sourced.
* **Only 2 of 6 nus8 cameras have measured spectral sensitivities** in camspec.
* **The Phase 2 diagnosis is incomplete.** It attributed the sclera's failure to inter-individual reflectance variance; Phase 2.5 removed that term entirely and the result did not improve (p=0.29), so something else contributes and has not been identified.
* **A deep model on raw waveforms was the last untested representation and is now tested.** Nothing further is proposed.

## 4. Corrections made during the project

Every one of these was caught by a check built into the work rather than by review. They are listed because the checks that caught them are reusable, and because a project whose contribution is demanding rigour of others should show its own error record.

| # | what was wrong | how it was caught | what changed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Haemoglobin extinction coefficients transcribed from memory were wrong: **18.8% of points >10% off, max 252%** (deoxy-Hb at 470 nm: 16,156 sourced vs 56,880 transcribed) | Downloaded primary sources in Phase 3.5 and diffed against the transcription | All spectral constants now load from `data/raw/optical_constants/`. Phase 3 gate re-run: 3.417 -> 3.893 g/dL, verdict unchanged |
| 2 | **The isosbestic self-check PASSED on that bad data.** It verified where the two curves cross - which was correct - while the values between the crossings were wrong | Comparing against the downloaded file, after the check had already passed | Recorded as a standing lesson: an internal consistency check is not a verification. The check is still useful - it caught two real transcription errors earlier (500 nm isosbestic at 515 nm; a collapsed 529/545 pair) |
| 3 | **Circularity trap**: measuring sclera self-consistency on the same pixels used to estimate the illuminant returns the prior exactly - spread collapses to zero by construction | Caught in design, before running, and then confirmed: on the circular measure the sclera methods appear to win (2.47 vs 3.33 dE2000); on the non-circular iris they lose | Iris became the primary evaluation region. A test asserts the degeneracy exists so nobody 'simplifies' the protocol back into it |
| 4 | **Unfair initialisation**: the deep regression head starts at 0 against a mean Hb of 13.9 g/dL, giving MAE 9.1 - which looks like catastrophic failure but is only an untrained intercept | Smoke-testing the deep pipeline at 3 epochs before committing to a full run | Target standardised per training fold. A negative result from an unconverged model would have been worthless |
| 5 | **SNR harmonics bug**: counting PPG harmonics as noise gave ~4.5 dB against the dataset's published 16-19 dB | Checking the extraction against the dataset's own published per-channel SNR | Cardiac power now includes 2f and 3f -> 8.2 dB. The published ordering still does not reproduce, and that is reported rather than tuned into agreement |
| 6 | **Percentile-only highlight detector**: the top 1% of a 350,000-pixel region is ~3,500 pixels whether or not a highlight exists, so it 'found' one in every image (97.3% usable) | Inspecting why the usable rate was implausibly high | Detection now requires >=3x the region median AND the top percentile. Honest rate 71.7%. A test asserts a flat synthetic iris yields zero detections |
| 7 | **Subject leakage in N1b**: the first run selected 35 subjects instead of 100, and those 35 were exactly the subjects the segmentation model trained on | Noticing the subject count in the run's own output | Indexed all 16,717 MOBIUS frames; run stopped and restarted |
| 8 | **SBVPI masks read from the alpha channel**, which is uniformly opaque, labelling entire images as one class | Class-fraction sanity check on loaded labels (pupil = 100% of image) | Masks now read from the colour channels |
| 9 | **Incompatible label spaces**: MOBIUS marks periocular skin black (= background), SBVPI gives it its own class. Training naively teaches the model that skin is simultaneously class 0 and class 4 | Inspecting per-dataset label statistics before training | A loss that merges background/periocular logits for MOBIUS frames only. Periocular IoU 0.357 -> 0.975 |
| 10 | **16-bit PNGs read as 8-bit**: PIL silently truncates the NUS linear PNGs, collapsing ~1,848 distinct values to 11 | Checking the PNG header bit depth against what the loader returned | `cv2.IMREAD_UNCHANGED` throughout. Dark-level subtraction verified per camera at the same time (12.04 -> 0.89 deg on Canon600D) |
| 11 | **Memory bug**: retaining full-resolution images and labels for ~900 frames would have needed >100 GB | Reading the code before launching the run | Whole-image statistics computed per frame, images discarded immediately |
| 12 | **camspec parsed 0 cameras**: the grid is 400-720 nm (33 points), not 400-700 (31) | Zero cameras returned from a file with 28 in it | Corrected grid; a test pins 28 cameras at 33 points |

## 5. The single positive claim, after hardening

| test | result |
| --- | --- |
| real MAE (seed-averaged) | **1.1124** g/dL |
| seed stability (10 seeds) | SD **0.0069**, range 0.0210 |
| permutation, selected model only (n=200) | empirical p = **0.0050**, z = -5.34 |
| **permutation, selection-aware (n=60)** | empirical p = **0.0164**, z = -5.02 |
| robustness: drop the best-performing decile | MAE 1.1124 -> 1.2258 |

### Verdict on the positive claim: **SURVIVES**

The selection-aware null - the full best-of-six procedure re-run inside every permutation - is the correct construction, and the claim clears it on an empirical p-value rather than a parametric assumption.

Three details worth stating precisely:

* **The selection correction was necessary and insufficient to explain the effect.** Taking the best of six configurations does buy something by chance: the selection-aware null mean (1.1938) sits below the single-model null (1.2009) by 0.0071 g/dL. That is real, and it is an order of magnitude smaller than the 0.0815 g/dL gap being explained.
* **p = 0.0164 is the empirical FLOOR at n=60**, not a measured value: zero of 60 permutations reached the real MAE, so the true p is bounded above by 1/(60+1). The parametric z of -5.02 is reported beside it and assumes a normal null, which the 200-permutation distribution supports but does not prove.
* **Seed variance cannot explain it.** The real-vs-null gap (0.0815) is 11.9x the seed SD (0.0069), and dropping the best-performing decile of subjects degrades the model rather than removing the effect - consistent with a weak signal spread across the cohort rather than a few lucky subjects.

**It remains clinically useless and must never be reported without that sentence attached.** The effect improves on predicting a constant by ~0.05 g/dL; sex alone beats it by six times that margin; and an estimator at MAE 1.12 g/dL separates no WHO severity band.

## 7. Reproducibility

* `scripts/reproduce_all.py` runs every stage end to end (~12 h full, ~22 min with `--fast`). `--list` prints the plan with per-stage runtimes.
* Seed **20260911** is frozen in `configs/phase1_splits.yaml`.
* Two stages are non-deterministic (cuDNN autotune): segmentation training reproduces sclera IoU to ~0.005, and the deep sweep reproduces MAE to ~0.01 - which is the measured seed SD, and an order of magnitude below the effects reported.
* `reports/dataset_manifest.md` lists every dataset, its source, licence status and how to obtain it. **8 of 10 licences are UNVERIFIED.**
* `data/` and `reports/figures/` are gitignored; no dataset file or dataset-derived image is tracked.
