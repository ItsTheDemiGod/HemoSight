### Observations from the code text - NOT harness verdicts

The harness ran on nothing, so it returned no verdict. The following are readings of the released code, offered with the scope caveat above and with no generalisation:

- **baselines/hue_score_svm.ipynb, baselines/mean_ann.ipynb** - `train_test_split(..., test_size=test_split)` with **no `random_state`**: the split is different on every run, so no reported number could be reproduced even with the arrays.
- **experiments/xgboost_linreg.ipynb** - Positional slices: linear regression fit on `X[:200]`, evaluated on `X[200:]` (11 rows); the XGBoost cell fits on `X[:110]` and evaluates on `X[:20]` - **20 rows drawn from the training rows**. Labels are scaled by an undocumented constant (`*20` on both sides of the MSE).
- **experiments/vanilla_cnn.ipynb** - The `DataLoader.sample()` method computes a positional 80/10/10 slice of the augmented folder and then draws `random.choice(os.listdir(path))` from the **whole** folder, so the 'train' and 'validation' draws come from the same pool; augmented copies (Flip/Rotate/Scale/Translation) of one photograph can appear in both. `wandb` is used without being imported.
- **experiments/transfer-learning/*.ipynb** - `random_state=101` is set. The notebooks depend on the PH2 and HAM10000 archives and on `Italy_new.xlsx`, none released, and on the Keras 2 API (`ImageDataGenerator`, `adam_v2`) with no pinned versions.

No demographic or population baseline appears in any notebook (one notebook plots Hgb against age and gender without fitting a baseline); India and Italy are pooled; no duplicate check appears; no notebook carries saved outputs, so there is no reported number to compare against.
